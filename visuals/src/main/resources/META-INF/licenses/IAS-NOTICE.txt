Arcanic opens IAS's own account screen. IAS handles browser authentication,
storage and switching. IAS accounts are separate from Arcanic launcher's accounts.
Users may replace the IAS nested jar in META-INF/jars with a compatible modified
version for their own use; this integration does not prohibit reverse engineering
needed to debug such modifications.
